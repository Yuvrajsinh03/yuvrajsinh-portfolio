How I work

My Process

How I work

01

Research & Immersion

I go deep on the domain before touching Figma. For regulated industries, that means reading compliance docs — not just conducting user interviews.

02

Systems Thinking

Every screen exists within a flow. I design the logic before the pixels — information architecture is where trust is built or quietly broken.

03

Prototype & Test

High-fidelity prototypes tested with real users — including compliance officers, not just end consumers. Edge cases are first-class citizens here.

04

Ship & Iterate

I stay in the product after launch. Metrics, feedback loops, and a bias toward shipping over endlessly perfecting.

The Person

Read me

I make complex, high-stakes workflows feel inevitable — not just usable.

7+ years across fintech, cybersecurity, and startup MVPs. Currently Senior Product Designer at ComplyStream, a London-based financial crime startup — turning dense, regulated workflows into experiences that feel clear, fast, and trustworthy.

I live at the intersection of regulation and human behaviour. That means KYC flows that don't feel like interrogations, CISO dashboards that communicate risk without manufacturing panic, and onboarding experiences that earn trust inside the first sixty seconds.

I've been the sole designer at early-stage startups — designing without a system, shipping without a QA team, and building conviction for design in rooms full of engineers and investors. Currently exploring what happens when AI meets compliance design.
