How I work

My Process

How I work

01

Research & Immersion

I go deep on the domain before touching Figma. For regulated industries, that means reading compliance docs — not just conducting user interviews.

02

Systems Thinking

Every screen exists within a flow. I design the logic before the pixels — information architecture is where trust is built or quietly broken.

03

Prototype & Test

High-fidelity prototypes tested with real users — including compliance officers, not just end consumers. Edge cases are first-class citizens here.

04

Ship & Iterate

I stay in the product after launch. Metrics, feedback loops, and a bias toward shipping over endlessly perfecting.

The Person

Read me

I've spent 7 years asking "but would a compliance officer actually use this?" in rooms where that question wasn't always welcome.

I'm a product designer who lives at the intersection of regulation and human behaviour. Most of my career has been in fintech and regtech — designing products that sit between financial institutions and the rules that govern them.

That means KYC flows that don't feel like interrogations. CISO dashboards that communicate risk without manufacturing panic. Onboarding experiences that earn trust inside the first sixty seconds.

I've been the sole designer at early-stage startups, which means I know what it's like to design without a design system, ship without a QA team, and build conviction for design decisions in rooms full of engineers and investors.

Currently exploring what happens when AI meets compliance design. Writing about it publicly. Building things that matter.
